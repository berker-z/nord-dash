Privacy Policy

nord-dash · last updated 27 August 2026

nord-dash is a personal dashboard operated by its owner for private use by a small, explicitly allowlisted set of Google accounts. It is not a commercial service and has no other users.

What it stores

  • Your Google account email, display name, and profile picture.
  • Google OAuth tokens (access and refresh) for each calendar account you connect, so the dashboard can read and edit your calendar on your behalf without asking you to sign in repeatedly.
  • The list of calendars in each connected account, and which you have chosen to display.
  • Content you create in the dashboard itself: to-do items and notes.

Where it is stored

In Google Firebase (Firestore), in a document keyed to your own email address. Database rules restrict every record so that it can only be read or written by the signed-in Google account it belongs to.

How Google data is used

Calendar data is requested solely to display your calendar and agenda inside the dashboard and to let you create, edit, and delete your own events. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train any model or serve any purpose beyond the features you see in the app.

nord-dash's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third parties

The dashboard calls Google (sign-in and Calendar) plus a small number of public data feeds for weather and cryptocurrency prices. Those feeds receive no personal information — only an anonymous request for public data. The site is hosted on Vercel.

Retention and removal

Disconnecting a calendar account inside the dashboard deletes its stored tokens and calendar configuration. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have any remaining data deleted, use the contact address below.
